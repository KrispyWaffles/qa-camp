# BUG-012 — Forgot password does nothing

**Environment:** macOS | Chrome | FoxAuth v1.0 (local)
**Severity:** Critical / Major / Minor / Trivial
**Priority:** High / Medium / Low

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open FoxAuth login form
2. Click "forgot password"

## Expected result
workflow to retrieve or create a new password

## Actual result
nothing happens

## Notes
Security issue - Users not an alternative option to get into their account in case the forgot or lost their password. Also, if the just want to change it, especially if they feel that their account was compromised.
