# BUG-010 — strength bar only updates on blur

**Environment:** macOS | Chrome | FoxAuth v1.0 (local)
**Severity:** Critical / Major / Minor / Trivial
**Priority:** High / Medium / Low

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open FoxAuth login form
2. enter a long character password. At least 6 characters
3. minimize the window 
4. click outside of the window

## Expected result
Strength bar should increase when entering a complex or long character password

## Actual result
Strength bar updates after clicking outside of the window

## Notes
Low impact - This can be an issue to know the strengh of your password. Also, a better one would require special characters for complexity and better security
